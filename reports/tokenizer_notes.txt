1. Tokens & Token IDs
Tokens: A token is a piece of a word, a whole word, or a punctuation mark. It is the fundamental unit of text that the model reads. For example, the word "unbelievable" might be split into three tokens: "un", "believ", and "able".

Token IDs: The model has a massive dictionary (vocabulary) of every possible token. Each token is assigned a unique integer called a Token ID. For example, "un" might be 482, "believ" might be 8493, and "able" might be 332.

2. Special Tokens
These are unique tokens added to the text to give the model structural instructions.

[CLS] (Classifier) / <s> (Start): Often added to the very beginning of a sequence to tell the model, 
           n                     "The text starts here."

[SEP] (Separator) / </s> (End): Added at the end of a sequence or used to separate two different sentences
                          (e.g., Question and Answer).

[PAD] (Padding): Used to fill up empty space (explained below).

3. Maximum Sequence Length
AI models are built with a fixed "context window" or memory limit. They process data 
in uniform batches, so they require an explicit Maximum Sequence Length (e.g., 512, 2048, or 8192 tokens).
Every piece of text fed into the model during a batch must fit this exact length.

4. Padding & Truncation
Because real-world sentences are all different lengths, we use padding and truncation
 to force them to match the Maximum Sequence Length.

Truncation: If a sentence is longer than the maximum length, the model chops off the
 end of it so it fits perfectly.

Padding: If a sentence is shorter than the maximum length, we add empty dummy tokens 
(usually [PAD], which has a Token ID of 0) to the end until it reaches the exact max length.

5. Attention Mask
When we add a bunch of [PAD] tokens to a short sentence, we introduce a problem: 
the model might try to "read" those padding tokens and think they are important.
The Attention Mask solves this. It is an array of 1s and 0s of the exact same length 
as the sequence:

1 means: "This is a real token, pay attention to it."

0 means: "This is a padding token, completely ignore it."
